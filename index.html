Four Modules

Color tells the story.
Texture makes it believable.

GRADE + GRAIN is built around four tightly integrated modules — each one addressing a different layer of the cinematic image, from the underlying grade to the final emulsion character.

01 / 04

AI Look Match

Analyze any reference frame and extract its cinematic signature. GRADE + GRAIN reads contrast, density, color balance and overall mood — then matches your footage to it, shot for shot.

02 / 04

Chroma Pulp™

The integrated grading core — exposure, highlight rolloff, shadow knee and creative veto controls, with reference matching built directly in. Push the AI's read, or override it shot by shot.

03 / 04

Look Signature

Analyze a reference frame's contrast and saturation signature, preview the result, and export it as a .cube LUT for use anywhere in your pipeline — with adjustable global blend.

04 / 04

Film Grain

Authentic motion picture grain, modeled from real emulsions. Kodak. Fuji. Custom stocks. Independent control over amount, size, halation and bloom.

Inside Resolve

Built for the suite professionals already live in.

No new windows, no roundtrips. GRADE + GRAIN runs natively as an OFX node — full reference matching, waveform-aware analysis and film-stock emulation, right inside your color page.

Beta Access

Licensed beta, built into the plugin.

Every install ships with a "License" group right in the Inspector — enter your key, hit Validate, and you're unlocked. The panel shows your activation status, days remaining until expiry, and the installed plugin version (currently 1.2), and you can switch to a new key at any time with "Change License".

Zipped Bundle
